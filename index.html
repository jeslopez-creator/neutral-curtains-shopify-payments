Shop Pay at checkout

One-tap checkout for the 9 in 10 shoppers who already use it.

▾
  • Shopify reports Shop Pay can lift conversion by as much as 50% compared to guest checkout, when it's used.
  • It outperforms other accelerated checkouts by at least 10%.
  • Checkout is up to 4x faster than guest checkout.
  • It recognizes most returning Shop Pay customers with no action from them, so they skip re-entering card, shipping and billing details.
  • Shopify reports over 100 million buyers already set up for one-click checkout with Shop Pay.
Source: Shopify Checkout research →
💳

Pay-over-time built in

Shop Pay Installments lets shoppers split payments right inside Shop Pay.

▾
  • Shoppers can split purchases from $50 to $999.99 into 2 or 4 interest-free payments, every 2 weeks.
  • Monthly plans are available on larger orders, with the order range depending on your package and eligibility.
  • You get paid in full, up front. Affirm collects the payments, so you aren't at risk if a customer stops paying.
  • Banners can show on your product and cart pages, so shoppers see the option before checkout.
  • Requires a US-based store selling in USD, with Shopify Payments and Shop Pay turned on.
Learn more about Shop Pay Installments →
🛡️

Fraud chargeback protection

Shopify Protect covers fraudulent chargebacks on eligible Shop Pay orders.

▾
  • On protected fraud-based chargebacks, Shopify covers the total order cost and the chargeback fee, and handles the dispute for you.
  • Described by Shopify as Shop Pay's free, built-in chargeback protection, at no extra cost.
  • Orders marked "protection active" are eligible and safe to fulfill.
  • To stay covered, fulfill eligible orders within 7 days with tracking from a supported carrier.
  • For US stores using Shopify Payments and Shop Pay. Terms and conditions apply.
Learn more about Shopify Protect →

Sales and cash flow in one view, inside Shopify.
